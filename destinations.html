COMMON QUESTIONS

Things students ask us.

Which destination is best for me?

It depends on your budget, academic profile and long-term goals. If PR is your priority, Canada is usually the strongest option. For a shorter, high-brand degree, the UK leads. For work opportunities during study, Australia offers the most hours. We help you compare all of this honestly in your first counselling session.

How much does it cost in total?

Beyond tuition, plan for living costs (roughly USD 800–1,500 per month depending on country and city), visa fees, health insurance, airfare and a one-time consultancy fee. We give every student a full cost breakdown before they commit to anything.

Can I work while I study?

Yes — every destination we support allows part-time work during study (usually 20 hours per week, 48 hours per fortnight in Australia). These hours are meant to help with living expenses, not to fund your entire education.

Do I need IELTS or TOEFL?

Most universities require proof of English proficiency. Some accept alternative tests, and a few waive the requirement if your previous education was in English. We'll tell you exactly what your shortlisted universities ask for.

How long does the whole process take?

Usually 3 to 6 months from your first counselling session to your flight. Visa processing alone can take 3 to 12 weeks depending on the country and time of year — so starting early matters.

What if my visa is rejected?

We prepare applications carefully to minimise this risk, and we review every financial document before submission. In the rare case of a refusal, we help you understand the reason and work through the next steps — reapplication, alternative destination, or deferral.
