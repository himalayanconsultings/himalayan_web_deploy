ABOUT HIMALAYAN CONSULTANCY

Built around people,

We believe choosing to study abroad is a major decision. Our role is to make the process clearer, more informed and more personal.

01 / OUR STORY

Your goals deserve personal attention.

Himalayan Consultancy is an education consultancy based in Nepal, helping students explore international study opportunities and prepare for the application journey.

From your very first counselling session to the day you board your flight, we work alongside you — reviewing your profile, matching you with the right institutions, and preparing every document carefully so nothing is rushed.

We believe good guidance is honest, personal and long-term. That's the standard we hold ourselves to with every student who walks through our door.
