SEVEN SERVICES

What we do — in detail.

Each service is delivered by a specialist. You'll always know who's handling your file and what happens next.

01

Career & Course Counselling

We start by understanding you — academic background, interests, budget and long-term goals. Then we map out course and career pathways that make sense.

  • One-on-one profile assessment
  • Course & career pathway mapping
  • Budget & family discussion
02

University & College Selection

Explore institutions that match your profile, budget and ambitions. We compare accreditation, location, tuition, and post-study opportunities.

  • Ranked shortlist based on your profile
  • Tuition & living cost comparison
  • Intake, deadline & requirement guidance
03

Application Assistance

Guidance with application preparation, documentation and submission — so nothing important is missed and every deadline is met with time to spare.

  • Application form filling & review
  • Course & intake confirmation
  • Offer letter follow-up with universities
04

Document Handling & Preparation

The most critical part of any application. We prepare, organize and verify every document an international application requires — checked and cross-checked.

  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) writing
  • CV & recommendation letters
  • Financial documents & bank statements
  • Passport, IELTS/TOEFL & test scores
  • Translation & notarization coordination
05

Scholarship & Financial Guidance

Understand what scholarships and funding options are available for your profile, and prepare stronger applications wherever you're eligible.

  • Merit & need-based scholarship search
  • University-specific funding applications
  • Sponsorship & bank loan documentation
06

Visa Guidance & Interview Prep

Organize the required documents and prepare for the visa process with mock interviews and honest, realistic timelines.

  • Country-specific visa documentation
  • Financial proof & sponsor review
  • Visa interview mock sessions
  • Embassy appointment scheduling
07

Pre-Departure & Post-Arrival Support

Practical information to help you settle in quickly — from travel and accommodation to banking and life as an international student.

  • Air ticket & travel insurance
  • Accommodation search & booking
  • Forex, banking & SIM card guidance
  • Airport pickup & first-week orientation
